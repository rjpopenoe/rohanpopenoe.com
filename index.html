About

I build technology that brings people closer together.

I'm a product manager and first-year MBA candidate at NYU Stern, specializing in tech product management and AI. I spent four years at RiskSpan, a structured-finance analytics firm, growing from junior analyst to product manager of its agentic AI platform. I pushed for AI early, while its value was still debated inside the company,.

Human connection is what drives my work. I believe technology, used with intention, can help people find each other again. Music does that better than almost anything, so in my free time I'm building a live listening space where people anywhere can share music together in real time.

My grandfather was an inventor who took his idea from prototype all the way to market. I carry his example with me, and I came to Stern to maximize my impact.

Outside work I'm training for my private pilot license. I also ride motorcycles, play guitar, ukulele and violin, cook, backpack, and look for live music anywhere I can find it in New York City.
